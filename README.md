# The FAO Science and Innovation Strategy and your work: course redesign concept

A portfolio piece by Erik Sootla: a full redesign of the FAO self-paced e-learning course on the FAO Science and Innovation Strategy.

- Three lessons (about 75 minutes) and a final check with one question per learning outcome
- Task-based outcomes for three job roles; plain-language text written from scratch
- Practice tasks with feedback: sorting, matching, branching scenarios, case studies
- Runs in any browser and as a SCORM 1.2 package (completion, score, pass/fail and bookmark are reported to the LMS)

Based on the FAO e-learning course on the FAO Science and Innovation Strategy (2025). Not an official FAO product.
